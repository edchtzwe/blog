## The AI Video Landscape (2026)

### What's Real
- [Object/scene detection — mature, useful for indexing]
- [Transcription and captioning — solved problem]
- [Basic frame interpolation — good enough for most use cases]

### What's Hype
- [AI "understanding" video — still pattern matching, not comprehension]
- [Generative video — impressive demos, unreliable in production]
- [AI-driven editing — works for simple cuts, fails on narrative structure]

### What Actually Matters
- [AI as a query layer: find relevant segments, don't generate new ones]
- [The real bottleneck: video files aren't designed for AI to manipulate them]
