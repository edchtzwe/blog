## Signs You Should Leave

### Financial Red Flags
- Payroll cycle changing (monthly → fortnightly)
- Vendors not getting paid
- "Equity instead of bonus" offers
- Cutting infrastructure, not optimising it

### Leadership Red Flags
- Directors with side hustles
- CEO doing US roadshows while the product is on fire
- "We're in talks with Meta" — said by every dying startup

### Engineering Red Flags
- No genuine R&D happening
- Only one person doing the actual experimental work (if that's you, you're the company)
- "AI" is a search wrapper
